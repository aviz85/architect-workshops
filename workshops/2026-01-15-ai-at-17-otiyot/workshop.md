# What If I Had AI at 17?

**Event:** Otiyot Writers Mini-Workshop
**Date:** Thursday, January 15, 2026
**Duration:** 10 minutes
**Audience:** Writers and journalists at Otiyot magazine
**Language:** Hebrew

---

## Core Message

**The Paradox:** If I had AI at 17, I probably wouldn't be a writer today. Writing was my survival mechanism - the only way I could process what I was going through. AI would have "solved" that problem before it became my identity.

**The Twist:** But NOW, after I know who I am and what I'm thirsty to bring to the world - AI doesn't replace me. It amplifies every spark. For someone with ADHD, every fleeting idea can now become a real product.

---

## Structure (10 minutes)

### 1. Opening Hook (2 min)
> "If I had AI at 17, I probably wouldn't be standing here today"

Provocative statement that grabs attention.

### 2. The Personal Story (3 min)
- Writing as survival mechanism at 17
- The intensity that forged identity as a writer
- AI would have "solved" the problem before it became a calling

### 3. The Pivot (2 min)
- After identity clarification, after the thirst is clear
- AI doesn't replace - it amplifies
- Every ADHD spark gets expression
- Idea → finished product in real time

### 4. Multimodal Demo (3 min)
Show how ONE spark becomes everything:
- Pre-made: Song (Suno), Video (Sora), Podcast (NotebookLM)
- Live: Text generation with Claude

---

## The Spark (Core Sentence)

> "בגיל 17 הייתי חייב לכתוב כדי לשרוד. היום, אחרי שאני יודע מי אני - הבינה המלאכותית לא מחליפה אותי, היא נותנת לכל ניצוץ שלי מקום להתקיים."

English translation for prompts:
> "At 17, I had to write to survive. Today, knowing who I am - AI doesn't replace me, it gives every spark of mine a place to exist."
