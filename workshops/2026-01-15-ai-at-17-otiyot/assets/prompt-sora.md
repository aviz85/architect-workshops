# Sora Prompt - Visual Journey

## Concept
A visual metaphor showing the transformation from isolated teenage writer to amplified creative force.

---

## Primary Prompt

```
A cinematic journey in two parts:

PART 1 (0:00-0:07): A teenager sits alone in a dim bedroom at night, writing intensely in a notebook by lamplight. Papers scattered everywhere. The mood is heavy, introspective. Rain on the window. Handheld camera, intimate.

PART 2 (0:07-0:15): Time dissolves. The same person, now adult, sits at a modern desk. As they type one sentence, visual sparks emerge from the screen - transforming into floating images, musical notes, video frames, podcast waveforms - all swirling around them in a beautiful constellation. The room fills with warm light. The person smiles, surrounded by their amplified creativity.

Style: Cinematic, emotional, slightly dreamlike. Color palette shifts from cold blue/gray to warm gold/green.
```

---

## Alternative - Simpler Version

If the complex version doesn't work well:

```
A single continuous shot: A glowing spark emerges from someone's chest (near the heart). The spark floats upward and multiplies into dozens of sparks. Each spark transforms into a different creative medium - one becomes text, one becomes a musical waveform, one becomes a video frame, one becomes a podcast icon. They all orbit around the person like a personal galaxy. Warm, hopeful lighting. Cinematic.
```

---

## Duration
15 seconds (will play full or loop during presentation)

## Aspect Ratio
16:9 (widescreen for presentation)
