# Claude Live Demo Prompt

## Setup
This is for the LIVE portion of the presentation. You'll type this into Claude while the audience watches.

---

## Option 1: Instant Article Angle

**Type this:**
```
I just gave a 7-minute talk to writers about this idea:

"At 17, I had to write to survive - it was my only way to process pain. If AI existed then, it would have 'solved' my problem before writing became my identity. But NOW, after I know who I am, AI amplifies every creative spark I have."

Give me 3 different article angles I could pitch to an editor tomorrow, each with a provocative headline and a 2-sentence pitch.
```

---

## Option 2: Flip the Perspective

**Type this:**
```
I'm a writer who believes AI would have prevented me from becoming a writer if I'd had it at 17 - because writing was how I survived, and AI would have "solved" that struggle.

Now argue the opposite. Play devil's advocate. Maybe AI at 17 would have made me an even BETTER writer. Give me 3 compelling arguments.
```

---

## Option 3: Personal & Immediate

**Type this:**
```
I have a spark right now: What if we interviewed teenagers who use AI to process their emotions? We could compare their creative development to people like me who had to struggle through it manually.

Turn this spark into:
1. A podcast episode outline
2. A research question
3. A personal essay opening paragraph
```

---

## Tips for Live Demo

1. **Talk while typing** - narrate what you're doing
2. **React authentically** - let surprise show if the output is good
3. **Don't read everything** - highlight one interesting part
4. **Time it** - aim for 30-45 seconds of Claude output, then move on

If internet fails, have a screenshot of a good Claude response ready in the assets folder.
