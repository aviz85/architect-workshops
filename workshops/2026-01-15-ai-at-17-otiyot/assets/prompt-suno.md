# Suno Prompt - "Sparks"

## Style
Indie folk, intimate, acoustic guitar, male vocals, reflective, building to hopeful

## Lyrics Concept (Let Suno generate based on this)

**Prompt:**
```
Create an emotional indie folk song about transformation and identity.

Theme: A person who had to write to survive as a teenager - writing was their only way to process pain. Now, years later, they've found their voice and AI amplifies every creative spark instead of replacing them.

Mood: Starts melancholic and intimate, builds to hopeful and empowered.

Key phrases to weave in:
- "I had to write to survive"
- "Every spark finds a place to exist"
- "Not replaced, amplified"

Style: Acoustic guitar, intimate male vocals, builds with light drums in chorus. Think Bon Iver meets Iron & Wine.

Language: English (or Hebrew if the tool supports it well)
```
