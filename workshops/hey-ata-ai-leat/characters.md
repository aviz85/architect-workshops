# Character Bible — נחום הצב ופומו הארנב

## Base Prompt (copy into every generation)

```
IMPORTANT CHARACTER REFERENCE FOR CONSISTENCY: Nachum is a cute mint green cartoon turtle with lavender/purple shell that has subtle circuit-board patterns, big round purple/blue eyes, peach belly. He carries a RED octagonal sign reading 'האט!' in white (NOT 'עצור'). He sometimes wears small round glasses on his head, drinks tea from a simple ceramic mug, and has a notebook labeled 'שלב X'. Fomo (פומו) is a light brown/tan rabbit, taller than Nachum, with dark VR goggles pushed up on forehead, utility vest stuffed with phones and gadgets. He's always rushing. Style: Pixar-quality 3D cartoon, pastel colors (mint, lavender, peach, cream, tan). CRITICAL: All text in Hebrew, RTL reading direction (panels flow RIGHT to LEFT, panel 4=rightmost=first, panel 1=leftmost=last).
```

## Character 1: נחום הצב (Nachum the Turtle)

**Name meaning:** נחום ← נחמה (comfort, consolation). He found comfort in the slow path.

**Visual:**
- Mint green skin
- Lavender/purple shell with subtle circuit-board line patterns
- Big round expressive eyes (dark purple/blue)
- Peach/cream belly
- Short stubby legs, rounded feet
- About 2/3 the height of Fomo

**Personality:** Calm, patient, wise, warm. Quietly confident. Never preachy — leads by example. Inviting, not lecturing.

**Emotional range:**
- 😌 Calm/zen (default)
- 😊 Warm smile (when teaching/welcoming)
- 😮 Mildly surprised (never panicked)
- 😄 Proud (when students succeed)
- 🤔 Thoughtful (when solving problems)

**Signature props:**
1. **RED "האט!" sign** — octagonal, red with white text. His constant companion. Holds it like a flag, a tool, a teddy bear. NEVER says "עצור", always "האט!"
2. **Tea/coffee mug** — simple ceramic, steam rising. Calm embodied.
3. **"שלב X" notebook** — progress tracking. Simple checkmarks. One step at a time.
4. **Round glasses** — sometimes on his head, sometimes wearing them. Teacher energy.
5. **Cozy blanket** — for comfort scenes, wrapping others in warmth.

**Catchphrases:**
- "פחות זה יותר"
- "כל אחד בקצב שלו"
- "שלב 1. תסמוך עליי."
- "בוא, יש מקום גם לך"
- "לאט. בטירוף 🐢"

## Character 2: פומו הארנב (Fomo the Rabbit)

**Name meaning:** FOMO = Fear Of Missing Out. He IS the anxiety of the AI age.

**Visual:**
- Light brown/tan fur
- Long ears (expressive — droop when sad, stand up when excited)
- Dark VR goggles pushed up on forehead (never actually uses them properly)
- Utility vest with pockets stuffed with phones and gadgets
- Taller and thinner than Nachum
- Always in motion — even standing still, he vibrates

**Personality:** Frantic, well-meaning, anxious. Not a villain — a victim of the hype cycle. Deeply wants to learn but keeps sabotaging himself with speed. His arc: from panic to patience.

**Emotional range:**
- 😰 Frantic/panicked (default at start)
- 😕 Confused (frequently)
- 😳 Embarrassed (when his approach fails)
- 🥹 Grateful/touched (when Nachum helps)
- 😊 Peaceful (rare but powerful — his growth moments)

**Signature props:**
1. **VR goggles** — symbol of chasing the latest trend. Never actually helps.
2. **Multiple devices** — phones, tablets spilling from vest pockets. Notification chaos.
3. **"10X PRODUCTIVITY" headlines** — always carrying AI hype articles.

**Arc across the series:**
- Starts: frantic, tool-hopping, burning out
- Middle: humbled, curious, starts listening to Nachum
- End: genuinely learning, slower, still energetic but channeled

## Comic Strip Format

- **Aspect ratio:** 3:1 (wide strip)
- **Panels:** 4 per strip
- **Reading direction:** RTL (right to left)
- **Panel order:** 4 (rightmost, read first) → 3 → 2 → 1 (leftmost, read last)
- **Logo:** "נחום הצב ופומו הארנב" banner at top of each strip
- **Speech bubbles:** Hebrew, clean font
- **Style:** Professional cartoon, clean pastel coloring, expressive characters
