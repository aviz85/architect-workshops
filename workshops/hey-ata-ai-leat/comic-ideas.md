# Nachum & Arnav Comic Strip Ideas

20 RTL comic strip concepts. All feature Nachum (turtle) and Arnav (rabbit).
Recurring props: red "האט!" sign, tea cup, "שלב X" notebook, VR goggles.

## Episodes

| # | Title | Synopsis |
|---|-------|----------|
| 6 | הפרומפט הראשון | Arnav writes a 500-word prompt. Nachum writes 5 words. Nachum's works better. "פחות זה יותר" |
| 7 | עדכון שבועי | Arnav installs 12 new AI tools every Monday. By Friday they're all uninstalled. Nachum still uses the same one from 3 months ago — and it does everything. |
| 8 | הלוד של הארנב | Arnav's phone has 847 AI app notifications. Nachum's phone has one app, no notifications, battery at 98%. "איך הסוללה שלך תמיד מלאה?" |
| 9 | הפגישה | Both present to a boss. Arnav's presentation has 40 AI-generated slides, none coherent. Nachum has 3 hand-picked slides. Boss picks Nachum. |
| 10 | FOMO אטאק | Arnav panics seeing a "Last chance to learn AI!" ad. Nachum calmly sticks his "האט!" sign over the ad. "אין 'הזדמנות אחרונה'. הטכנולוגיה לא בורחת." |
| 11 | קופי פייסט | Arnav copy-pastes AI output without reading. Gets embarrassed when it's wrong. Nachum reads, edits, understands. "AI זה עוזר, לא מחליף" |
| 12 | הלילה הלבן | Arnav pulls an all-nighter learning 5 frameworks. Next day can't remember any. Nachum slept 8 hours, learned one thing well. "שינה זה חלק מהלמידה" |
| 13 | הוובינר | Both attend an AI webinar. Arnav takes 200 screenshots. Nachum writes 3 notes in his notebook. A week later only Nachum remembers the content. |
| 14 | הבאג | Both hit a bug. Arnav asks 5 different AI tools frantically. Nachum reads the error message, thinks for a minute, fixes it himself. "לפעמים התשובה פשוטה" |
| 15 | חבר חדש | A new character appears — a snail who's even slower than Nachum. Nachum welcomes him warmly. Arnav: "רגע, יש עוד יותר איטי ממך?!" Nachum: "כל אחד בקצב שלו" |
| 16 | הדמו | Arnav builds impressive demo in 2 hours. It crashes live. Nachum's simple demo works perfectly. "עדיף פשוט שעובד" |
| 17 | רשימת המשימות | Arnav's AI-generated todo list has 47 items. Nachum's has 3. End of day: Nachum finished all 3, Arnav finished 0 of 47. |
| 18 | הפודקאסט | Arnav listens to 10 AI podcasts on 2x speed simultaneously. Nachum listens to one episode at normal speed, then applies what he learned. |
| 19 | שבת | Arnav can't stop checking AI news on Saturday. Nachum's phone is off, he's reading a book in the garden. Monday — Nachum is refreshed, Arnav is burned out. |
| 20 | בניית הסקיל הראשון | Nachum teaches Arnav to build his first skill. Arnav wants to start with the hardest thing. Nachum: "שלב 1. תסמוך עליי." By the end — Arnav's first skill works. Mind blown. |
| 21 | Context Window | Arnav dumps his entire life story into one prompt. AI gets confused. Nachum gives focused, relevant context. "תדמיין שאתה מסביר לעובד חדש — לא את כל חייך" |
| 22 | העתקת הגדולים | Arnav tries to replicate exactly what a tech influencer does. Fails because his context is different. Nachum builds something small that fits HIS life. "אל תהיה הוא. תהיה אתה." |
| 23 | הכיסא הריק | Nachum saves a chair in his classroom — labeled "הכיסא שלך". Different animals peek through the door in each panel. Last panel: the classroom is full. All learning slowly. |
| 24 | פסגת ההר | Split: Arnav takes a helicopter to the top of "AI Mountain" but there's nothing there — just fog. Nachum climbs step by step, sees every view along the way. Reaches a lower peak but enjoys the panorama. |
| 25 | המכתב | Nachum writes a letter to his past self: "אל תיבהל. זה ייקח זמן. וזה בסדר." Arnav reads it over his shoulder and tears up. "אתה כותב גם לי?" Nachum: "לכולנו 🐢" |
