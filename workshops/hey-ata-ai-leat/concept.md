# היי אתה! ללמוד AI לאט בטירווווף

## Status: Concept

## The Name — Layers of Meaning

"היי אתה" is a multi-layered wordplay:

1. **"היי אתה" ≈ "האטה"** — Sounds like "slowing down" in Hebrew. The core promise: slow, steady, grounded AI learning.
2. **"אתה" = You** — Personal presence. Pay attention to *yourself*. Don't lose who you are in the AI race.
3. **"בטירוף" flipped** — Everyone screams "AI is מטורף!" (crazy/amazing). This takes the same word from the opposite direction: the *learning* is insanely effective precisely *because* it's slow.
4. **Subversive message** — Sharp and counter-cultural: we must protect ourselves in this story. Fight for ourselves. Not get swept away.

## Core Philosophy

> "ללמוד AI לאט בטירווווף" — Learn AI slowly, insanely well.

The AI world pushes speed, urgency, FOMO. "You're already behind!" This program says the opposite:

- **Slow is the shortcut.** Rushing creates the "Tom Pattern" — skipping foundations, burning out, getting nothing.
- **Layers compound.** Each skill becomes the foundation for the next. Exponential efficiency through patience.
- **Presence over panic.** The goal isn't to know every tool — it's to *become* someone who can use any tool.
- **Human first, AI second.** The person doesn't serve the technology. The technology serves the person.

## Mascot: נחום הצב

**Name:** נחום (Nachum) — from the root נ.ח.מ = comfort, consolation (נחמה)

**Character arc:** נחום is exhausted. He's terrified. The AI world is moving too fast and he can't keep up. But then he finds נחמה — comfort — in the slow path. He realizes: being a turtle isn't a bug, it's a feature.

**נחום = the user.** Everyone who joins this program IS Nachum. Overwhelmed, scared, but ready to find their own pace.

**Emotional states:**
- 😫 מותש — Exhausted from trying to keep up with every new AI tool
- 😱 נבהל — Scared by the pace, the FOMO, the "you're already behind" messages
- 😌 שמח שמצא נחמה — Found peace in the slow approach. Relief. Home.

**Visual identity:**
- Mint green cartoon turtle, lavender/purple shell with circuit-board patterns
- Big expressive eyes, exaggerated shocked expressions (mouth wide open)
- Pastel palette: mint, lavender, peach, baby blue
- Props: stop sign ("האט!"), coffee cup, headphones, phone
- Style: Pixar-quality 3D cartoon, cute but tech-aware

**Why a turtle:**
- Naturally slow — it's not a choice, it's who he is
- Protected (shell = boundaries against the noise)
- Ancient wisdom — turtles have survived millions of years by being steady
- "Slow and steady wins the race" — but updated for the AI age
