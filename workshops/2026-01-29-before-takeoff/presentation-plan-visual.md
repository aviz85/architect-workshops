# Presentation Blueprint: קלודוש - רגע לפני השיגור

**Language:** Hebrew (עברית)
**Layout Direction:** RTL (Right-to-Left)
**Duration:** ~2 hours
**Date:** 2026-01-29 | 21:00
**Style:** EXTREME CLARITY - One word/phrase per slide, screaming visuals, emotional hooks

---

## Design System

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Assistant ExtraBold, 80-120pt, white or green (#22C55E)
- **Subheadlines:** Assistant Regular, 36pt, gray (#AAAAAA)
- **NO body text** - headlines only!

### Colors
- **Background:** Dark gradient (Navy #0F172A → Black #000000)
- **Primary accent:** Green #22C55E
- **Negative:** Red #EF4444
- **Positive:** Green #22C55E
- **Highlight:** Yellow #FACC15

### Visual Style
- Cinematic, futuristic, space theme
- Bold, exaggerated illustrations
- Strong emotional hooks
- Boss character (3D avatar in dark suit, green tie) appears throughout

### Slide Timing
- 15-30 seconds per slide
- Fast transitions
- Let images speak
