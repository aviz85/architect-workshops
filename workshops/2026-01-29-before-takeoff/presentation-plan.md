**Language:** Hebrew (עברית)
**Layout Direction:** RTL (Right-to-Left)
**Duration:** ~2 hours (120 minutes)
**Date:** 2026-01-29 | 21:00
**Concept:** "דגים + חכות" - Immediate value (fish) + Long-term tools (fishing rod)
**Special Note:** Last live workshop before switching to recorded format

## Presentation Overview

**Narrative Arc:**
1. **פתיחה** - The Urgency: Last Chance to Board (5 min)
2. **🎯 קלודביליות לייב** - Hook them with THEIR potential (15 min) ⭐ NEW!
3. **הבנת העקרון** - Why Claude Code is Different (15 min)
4. **הדג הראשון** - Immediate Win: Do Something Real Together (30 min)
5. **מסע בחללית** - Vision: What's Possible (20 min)
6. **ערכת החכות** - The Toolkit: Your Bridge to Independence (20 min)
7. **סיום** - Q&A, Giveaway, Next Steps (15 min)

**Visual Tone:** Cinematic, futuristic, space/rocket theme with green (#22C55E) accents. Boss character in astronaut helmet. Urgency + excitement.

**Typography (All Slides):**
- Headlines: Assistant Bold (Hebrew-optimized), 54pt, white or green (#22C55E)
- Body text: Assistant Regular, 24pt, light gray (#E5E5E5)
- Annotations: Assistant Light, 18pt, gray (#999999)
- Background: Dark gradient (navy #0F172A to black #000000)
