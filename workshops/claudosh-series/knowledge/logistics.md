# Logistics & Business Model

> Source: Deep Interview, 2026-02-15

## Schedule
- **Cadence:** Once a week (1 session per week)
- **Duration:** 4 weeks total
- **Session length:** 2 hours each
- **Total hours:** 8 hours of content

## Enrollment Model
- **Fixed cohort** - everyone starts session 1 together, progresses as a group
- No rolling enrollment (unlike the old 9-day cycle model)
- Homework between sessions builds on previous week

## Pricing
- **Target:** ₪497+ (premium positioning)
- Old 3-session series was ₪297
- More content, more depth, higher value justifies premium
