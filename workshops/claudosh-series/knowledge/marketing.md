# Marketing & Positioning

> Source: Deep Interview, 2026-02-15

## Core Hook
**מאפס לצבא סוכנים** (From Zero to Agent Army)

## Value Proposition
```
4 מפגשים | 8 שעות | מערכת הפעלה אישית

מפגש 1: מתקינים סוכן שמכיר אותך 🚀
מפגש 2: בונים ערימות של סקילים 🛸
מפגש 3: הסוכן הופך לאפליקציה 🛰️
מפגש 4: צבא סוכנים בשליטתך 🌌
```

## Target Audience
- Early Adopters
- No coding required
- Tech-curious, willing to learn
- Business owners, freelancers, professionals who work on computer
- "People who are ready to leave their comfort zone"

## Key Selling Points
1. **Not coding** - building a personal operating system
2. **Hands-on every session** - 60%+ practice
3. **Accumulative** - each session builds on previous, compounding effect
4. **Real business value** - CRM, WhatsApp, client communication
5. **Community** - ongoing support, alumni group, monthly sessions
6. **The full package** - recordings, guides, skill library, templates

## Pricing
- **₪497+** (premium positioning, 4 sessions × 2 hours)
- Justification: 8 hours of content + materials + community + ongoing support

## Differentiators
1. Hebrew - only serious Claude Code training in Hebrew
2. Practice-heavy - not theory, build real things
3. The leash model - safe, gradual progression
4. Full ecosystem - not just one tool, a complete system
5. Aviz's real system shown live - not toy demos

## Launch
- **Pilot beta group starting 2026-02-16**
- First cohort = test group, gather feedback, refine content
