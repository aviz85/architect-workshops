# Avatar Baby Boss - Character Sheet

> Baby version of Avatar Boss - same visual identity, infant proportions

## Reference

Based on: `avatar-boss.png` (same directory)

## Visual Style

- **Art Style:** 3D Pixar/DreamWorks animation style (like "Boss Baby" movie)
- **Render Quality:** High-quality CGI, smooth skin, subsurface scattering
- **Background:** Solid black (for compositing flexibility)

## Character Description

### Face & Head
- **Age appearance:** Baby/infant (8-12 months look)
- **Head shape:** Large, round baby head with soft features
- **Eyes:** Big, expressive brown/hazel eyes (same color as adult version)
- **Eyebrows:** Thick, dark, expressive (key recognizable feature)
- **Nose:** Small, cute baby nose
- **Mouth:** Small with knowing smirk/confident smile
- **Cheeks:** Chubby baby cheeks, rosy
- **Facial hair:** NONE (he's a baby!) - but keeps the confident boss expression
- **Hair:** Dark hair with subtle gray streaks, slicked back in same style as adult (signature look)

### Body & Proportions
- **Build:** Chubby baby body, short limbs
- **Height:** Baby proportions (large head relative to body)
- **Posture:** Confident, boss-like stance despite being a baby

### Outfit (CRITICAL - Same as Adult)
- **Suit:** Dark charcoal/black tailored suit (baby-sized, perfectly fitted)
- **Shirt:** Crisp white dress shirt
- **Tie:** Bright green tie (#22C55E) - THE signature brand color
- **Pocket square:** Green pocket square matching the tie
- **Shoes:** Tiny brown leather dress shoes (or bare baby feet in casual poses)

### Personality Traits to Convey
- Confident and in control
- Wise beyond his years
- Professional but adorable
- "I know things you don't" expression
- Commanding presence despite tiny size

## Pose Variations for Character Sheet

Generate as a **character sheet with multiple views**:

1. **Full body front** - Standing confidently, hands at sides or in pockets
2. **Full body side/3/4** - Profile view showing suit details
3. **Face close-up** - Showing the confident smirk and expressive eyes
4. **Face extreme close-up** - Eyes and expression detail
5. **Action pose** - Pointing finger up (like explaining something) or arms crossed

## Prompt Template

```
3D Pixar style character sheet, multiple views, baby in tailored dark suit with bright green tie and green pocket square, white dress shirt, slicked back dark hair with gray streaks, big expressive brown eyes, thick dark eyebrows, chubby baby cheeks, confident knowing smirk, professional boss baby aesthetic, solid black background, high quality CGI render, cute but commanding presence, full body front view, side view, face closeup, multiple angles
```

## Usage Notes

- Use for "beginner" or "intro" workshop themes
- Pairs well with: learning themes, "first steps", accessibility, friendly approachable content
- Represents: "Even a baby can do this" / "Start from the beginning"
- Contrast with adult avatar-boss for "advanced" vs "beginner" workshops
