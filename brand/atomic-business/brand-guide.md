# AVIZ — Atomic Business Brand Guide

## Brand Hierarchy

- **AVIZ** = parent brand (personal brand)
- **עסק אטומי** (Atomic Business) = product line / recurring workshop format
- Logo always appears as AVIZ, never "עסק אטומי" alone

## Logo

Two versions of the AVIZ wordmark:

| Version | File | Use |
|---------|------|-----|
| Dark | `aviz-logo-dark.svg` | Light backgrounds (#FAFAFA, white) |
| Light | `aviz-logo-light.svg` | Dark backgrounds (#22262D) |

Logo features: hand-drawn typographic style, rounded stroke-caps, cyan accent on the A's inner stroke and the I's dot.

## Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Role |
|------|-----|------|
| **Ink** | `#22262D` | Primary text, structure, logo |
| **Cyan** | `#11CFD0` | Brand accent, logo element |
| **Crimson** | `#B91C1C` | The Atomic Dot — nucleus accent |
| **Canvas** | `#FAFAFA` | Background, breathing room |
| **Mist** | `#E8E8E8` | Subtle lines, dividers |

### Color Rules

- Background is always light (Canvas or white)
- Cyan is used sparingly — logo and occasional accents
- Crimson appears ONLY as The Atomic Dot — never as fills, backgrounds, or large elements
- Ink for all text. Use lighter weights (#888, #AAA) for secondary text

## The Atomic Dot

A single crimson (#B91C1C) circle — the signature brand element.

**What it represents:** The nucleus. A small core containing immense energy. The essence of "atomic business" — one person, infinite potential.

**How it's used:**
- As a period/full stop after titles: עסק אטומי●
- As a standalone brand mark
- As a divider between elements
- Always crimson. Always circular. Always ONE.

**Parallel to the logo:** The cyan dot on the I = AVIZ identity. The crimson dot = atomic business energy.

## Typography

**Font:** Heebo (Google Fonts)

| Weight | Use |
|--------|-----|
| 900 (Black) | Headlines, titles |
| 700 (Bold) | Subtitles, key phrases |
| 400 (Regular) | Body text, descriptions |
| 300 (Light) | Details, metadata, secondary info |

**Spacing:** Generous letter-spacing on light weights. Tight on headlines.

## Visual Language

### DO
- Lots of white space — 60%+ of any composition
- Concentric circles / ripples radiating from center (expansion metaphor)
- Clean, minimal compositions
- Let typography do the heavy lifting

### DON'T
- No neon, no glow effects
- No dark backgrounds as default (dark variant exists but is secondary)
- No atomic/physics/electron imagery
- No tech/circuit/digital patterns
- No busy compositions

## Brand Feeling

The brand communicates all four simultaneously:
1. **Calm confidence** — "I have this handled"
2. **Freedom & lightness** — spacious, unburdened
3. **Quiet revolution** — understated but transformative
4. **Premium exclusivity** — curated, for serious people

## Core Message

**גרעין קטן · פוטנציאל אינסופי**
(Small nucleus · Infinite potential)

The atomic business metaphor is about ENERGY and EXPANSION — not physics. It's about freedom, productivity, beauty, and the disproportionate power that agents give a small business to scale without limits.
