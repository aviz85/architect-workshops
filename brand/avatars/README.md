## When to Use Which

**Real photo (`aviz-photo.png`)** — when authenticity matters more than aspiration:
- Personal stories, vulnerable posts, lessons learned
- Bio sections, "about" pages
- Lectures where you're framed as a teacher, not a brand

**Boss avatar (`avatar-classic.jpg` / `avatar-boss.png`)** — when projecting the brand:
- Workshop promo posters
- "Power" content (escalation, results, ambition)
- Anything where the green-tie suit reinforces the Architect identity

**Baby Boss (`avatar-baby-boss.jpg`)** — when softening the brand:
- Beginner/onboarding content ("start small")
- Humor, playful angles
- "Even a kid can do this" framing

## Brand Colors

- Primary green: `#22C55E`
- Accent: green tie / green pocket square (matches primary)
- Backgrounds: dark navy/black for premium, hot pink (#FF1F8F-ish) for personal/edgy
